OpenClaw Daily Digest Bot

A self-hosted AI agent running in Docker that delivers a personalized daily briefing through Discord and email every morning without any manual input. Procedural Music Generator

A web app that composes short pieces and shows them as real sheet music. I rebuilt it from an old Python genetic algorithm into a rule-based composer that writes in phrases, develops motifs, and follows chord progressions, then engraves the result on a grand staff and plays it back with a built-in synth. Pick a scene or a key and tempo, choose from more than a dozen instrument voices, and export to MIDI.
